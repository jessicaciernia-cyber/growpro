# Loft — Family Room Redesign

AI-staged redesign concept for the upstairs loft, styled as a **warm modern
family living room**. Generated with the `banana` skill (Google Gemini Nano
Banana image editing) from the original room photos.

## Confirmed layout
- **TV** wall-mounted on the **long wall** with a low walnut media console beneath.
- **Sectional sofa** placed against the **half-wall railing over the staircase**,
  running the length of that railing (back to the stairs), **facing the TV**.
- **Coffee table** on a large jute rug in the middle, between sofa and TV.
- The room's single **window** stays on the back-center wall; a reading chair and
  a fiddle-leaf fig sit in the window corner.

The two "living room" renders show this same layout from opposite directions
(from the stairs, and from the bedroom doorway).

## Design language
White-oak wide-plank flooring · ivory jute rug · oatmeal boucle sectional ·
walnut media console · round travertine coffee table · rattan accent chair ·
brushed brass accents · fiddle-leaf fig + trailing pothos · warm 2700K lighting.
